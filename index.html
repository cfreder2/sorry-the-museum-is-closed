About

Sorry, the museum is closed.

This is the result of experimentation with agents and APIs after teaching some of my students about how APIs work. This game uses the Art Institute of Chicago's open collection data and their live API to show you what art is in each gallery in their collection. I'll do my best to place the art in a room where the art would, in fact, show up. There is no specific information on where they hang exactly, so we make our best guess.

Additionally, this game uses the Open Library API to fill the gift shop and other areas with books about the artists and time periods. It also uses Creative Commons and non-copyrighted films about art and shows them in the various theater areas throughout the museum.

Enjoy exploring the museum and its grounds, have fun, watch out for the security guards, and see what secrets and trouble you can uncover!

The game sends anonymous play statistics: how long, how you got in, which rooms. No name and nothing personal. You can turn it off in Settings.
